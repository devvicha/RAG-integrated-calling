# Wasana Bank Pawning Services (ආභරණ ණය සේවාව)

## What is Pawning? (පෝනිං/ආභරණ ණය කියන්නේ මොකක්ද?)

Pawning (also called jewellery loan or gold loan) is a secured loan service where you can get instant cash by pledging your gold or gold jewellery items as collateral. You don't need to sell your valuables - you simply pledge them temporarily and get them back after repaying the loan.

**Sinhala Terms:**
- පෝනිං (Pawning)
- පවුන් (Pawn)
- ආභරණ ණය (Jewellery Loan)
- රන් ණය (Gold Loan)
- ගිරවීම (Pledging)
- ගිරවා ගන්න (To pledge/pawn)

## Common Questions (නිතර අසන ප්‍රශ්න)

### Q: How do I get a pawning loan? (පෝනින් ණයක් ගන්නේ කොහොමද?)
**A:** Visit any Wasana Bank branch with your gold jewellery and valid ID. We'll test the purity, calculate the loan amount, and give you instant cash.

### Q: What items can I pawn? (මොනවද ගිරවන්න පුළුවන්?)
**A:** Gold jewellery items (22K or 24K). The loan amount depends on weight and purity.

### Q: Do I need a guarantor? (ගැරන්ටර් කෙනෙක් ඕනේද?)
**A:** No, you don't need any guarantor for pawning loans.

### Q: How is the loan amount calculated? (ණය මුදල කොහොමද තීරණ වෙන්නේ?)
**A:** Based on the weight and purity of your gold items. A maximum amount is provided for a Sovereign (8g) of 22K/24K gold.

### Q: Is my jewellery safe? (මගේ ආභරණ ආරක්ෂිතද?)
**A:** Yes! Your items are stored with maximum security and full confidentiality. We guarantee the safety of your valuables.

### Q: How do I get my jewellery back? (මගේ ආභරණ ආපහු ගන්නේ කොහොමද?)
**A:** Once you repay the loan in full (either at maturity or through installments), your jewellery will be returned to you immediately.

### Q: Can I pay in installments? (වාර වශයෙන් ගෙවන්න පුළුවන්ද?)
**A:** Yes! You can pay through any Wasana Bank branch or via Wasana Vishwa online banking.

### Q: What are the interest rates? (පොලී අනුපාත මොනවද?)
**A:** Competitive rates are offered based on loan amount and tenure. Contact your nearest branch for current rates.
